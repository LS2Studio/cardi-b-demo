The Album

Seven years later, she answered.

Am I The Drama? arrived September 19, 2025 on Atlantic Records, her first studio album since Invasion of Privacy in 2018. The title borrows an iconic RuPaul's Drag Race line from Scarlet Envy. The cover puts Cardi in a red bodysuit surrounded by black crows, and the crows became the visual signature of the whole era.

The Story

From the Bronx to the record books.

Belcalis Marlenis Almanzar went from viral videos to the biggest stages in music in under a decade, and she did it on her own terms.

2015

Love & Hip Hop: New York

Joins the VH1 reality series in season six and becomes its breakout star, turning a viral social following into a national audience.

2016

First mixtape

Releases Gangsta Bitch Music, Vol. 1, her debut mixtape, and follows it with Vol. 2 in 2017.

2017

Bodak Yellow hits No. 1

Bodak Yellow tops the Billboard Hot 100, making her the first solo female rapper to reach No. 1 since Lauryn Hill in 1998.

2018

Invasion of Privacy, and a Grammy

Her debut studio album drops and wins Best Rap Album at the Grammys, making her the first solo woman ever to win that category. It becomes the most streamed female rap album on Spotify and Apple Music.

2019

Hustlers and Rhythm + Flow

Makes her film debut as Diamond in Hustlers opposite Jennifer Lopez, and judges and executive produces Netflix’s Rhythm + Flow with T.I. and Chance the Rapper.

2020

WAP breaks the internet

WAP with Megan Thee Stallion debuts at No. 1 on the Hot 100 and becomes a cultural event.

2021

Up, F9, and Whipshots

Up hits No. 1, she joins the Fast & Furious franchise as Leysa in F9, and co-founds Whipshots, her vodka-infused whipped cream brand.

2022

Playboy creative director

Becomes creative director in residence at Playboy and a founding creative director for its Centerfold platform.

2025

Am I The Drama?

After seven years, the second album arrives September 19 and debuts at No. 1 on the Billboard 200. She also hosts WWE SummerSlam.

2026

The tour, the hair line, the Super Bowl

The Little Miss Drama Tour runs February through April and becomes the highest grossing arena tour by a female rapper in history. Grow-Good Beauty launches in March. She performs in the Super Bowl LX halftime show with Bad Bunny and Karol G.

The Business

Not endorsements. Ownership.

Cardi B’s portfolio has shifted from paid partnerships to equity. She owns pieces of the brands she builds, with an estimated net worth of $120M (Celebrity Net Worth) and a music catalog valued around $70M.

Co-Founder • 2021

Whipshots

Vodka-infused whipped cream, co-founded with Starco Brands. The first million cans sold in 10 months, and the brand passed 5 million cans within three years. Flavors include vanilla, caramel, mocha, lime, chocolate, and peppermint.

Unlike a paid ad, Whipshots is hers. She holds equity and profits from every can.

Joint Venture • 2025

Revolve Group

Her first fully owned apparel and beauty brands, built through a joint venture with Revolve Group. After years of campaigns for Fashion Nova, Reebok, and Balenciaga, this is the move from endorser to founder.
